Privacy Policy

Havineini app and website (shiurim of Rabbi Shimon Spitzer) · Last updated November 2026

What the app does

The Havineini app and website play and download Torah shiurim and show their source pages. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, send or store any personal information on our side. There are no ads and no analytics.

Information kept on your phone

Your listening history, where you stopped, favorites, My Shiurim, My Queue, downloads and settings are stored only on your phone (or, on the website, only in your browser). They are never sent to us.

Reminders

The daily reminder is set on your phone and does not send any information anywhere.

Content

Shiurim, the episode list and source pages are loaded from other websites (such as Libsyn, GitHub and HebrewBooks). Like any website, these see standard technical information such as your IP address when the app loads files from them.

Deleting your information

Since everything is stored only on your phone, deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) removes all of it.
