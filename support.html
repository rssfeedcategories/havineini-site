Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in; everything works right away.
  • Listen offline: tap the download button on any shiur.
  • Keep my place in a series: add it to My Shiurim (long-press a shiur, or the My Shiurim button on the chooser).
  • Line up shiurim: long-press a shiur → Play next or Add to My Queue.
  • See the page of the sefer: open a halacha or Daf Yomi shiur and tap View PDF.
